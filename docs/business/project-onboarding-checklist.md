# New website project onboarding

Uses the policies approved November 16, 2026. Keep actual client records and credentials in the private project system, not this public source repository.

## Before accepting the deposit

- Identify the client's decision maker, project goal, actual pages/functions, platform, and editing needs.
- Complete the proposal and agreement. State the total price, 50% deposit, and 50% balance after approval but before launch/handover, or the agreed milestone schedule.
- Confirm two consolidated revision rounds and separately approved pricing/timing for added scope. Implementation corrections do not consume a round.
- Identify exactly what files, administrative access, custom-work rights, third-party licenses, and recurring dependencies the handover covers.
- Explain the 30-calendar-day post-launch window for reporting scoped defects, the correction process, and the difference from ongoing changes or care.
- Set content/access deadlines, a review schedule, and the launch dependencies. Record the client-approved scope and agreement version.
- Keep the domain and business accounts client-controlled wherever possible. Use secure access invitations rather than collecting passwords through the public inquiry form.

## During the build

- Track content and account dependencies; after 10 business days without required input, remind the client and agree a revised schedule.
- Collect each revision round as one feedback list and keep a record of the agreed changes.
- Quote and obtain written approval for added scope before beginning it.
- Check responsive layouts, links, forms and actual receipt/reply-to, booking flows if included, metadata, redirects, and agreed analytics. Use clearly marked test data with the client's involvement.

## Approval, payment, and launch

- Obtain the client's approval against the written scope.
- Confirm full payment before launch or handover.
- Deliver the agreed site files and access, hosting/editing instructions, integration dependencies, and transfer information.
- Record the actual launch date and the deadline for reporting defects under the 30-day window. Give the client a clear way to report a reproducible problem.
- Confirm any chosen ongoing hosting/care plan and its written scope. Record declined care as well; it is optional.

## Follow-up

- Correct reported defects against the approved scope. Quote new features, content changes, and other work separately.
- Ask for honest feedback and, where appropriate, permission for a portfolio entry, testimonial, or measured case study. A review request does not include a reward or a requested rating.
- Record received inquiries, consultations, quotes, and wins in the private lead workflow. Use the inquiry reference to reconcile messages without putting personal details into analytics or this repository.
