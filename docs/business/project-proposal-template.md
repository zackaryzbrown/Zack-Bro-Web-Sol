## Reviews and changes

The build includes two consolidated revision rounds within the approved scope. Each round is one collected list of feedback. Corrections where the build does not match the agreed scope do not use a revision round.

New pages, features, or a change of direction are quoted separately. The client approves the added price and schedule before that work begins. Record the date and scope of each revision round and change request.

## Defect fixes and ongoing care

Each website build includes 30 calendar days after launch to report reproducible defects against the approved scope, which Zackary corrects. New features, content changes, client edits, and third-party outages are outside that defect-fix scope.

Record the launch date and resulting reporting deadline when the site goes live. Hosting and ongoing care are optional. If selected, attach the actual plan scope, edit allowance, price, billing, and cancellation/transfer arrangements. Do not imply that maintenance includes unlimited edits or round-the-clock support.

## Before sending

Confirm the parties, scope, numbers, dates, third-party costs, cancellation/refund terms, rights, and any required agreement provisions. Remove every placeholder, verify consistency with the sales pages, and keep the accepted version with the client's project records. Existing signed client agreements are not replaced by this template.
